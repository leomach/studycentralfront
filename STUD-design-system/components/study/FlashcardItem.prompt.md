The session's flashcard screen. The front is display type; the back switches to Instrument Serif — the editorial contrast that marks "answer".

```jsx
<FlashcardItem flashcard={fc} meta="Pergunta · Direito › Tributário" intervals={{1:1,2:3,3:8,4:14}} onGrade={grade} />
```

`kind: "resumo"` collapses grading to two buttons ("Preciso rever" / "Revisei").
