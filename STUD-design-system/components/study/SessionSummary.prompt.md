Shown when the queue empties or the timer ends.

```jsx
<SessionSummary stats={{questionsAnswered:14,questionsCorrect:11,cardsReviewed:9,elapsedSeconds:2400,subjects:["Licitações","Concordância"]}} onFinish={goHome} />
```

Report the numbers plainly and encouragingly — no confetti, no scores, no streak fireworks.
